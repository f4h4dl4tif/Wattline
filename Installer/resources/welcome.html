Wattline

Your Mac's battery, finally explained. Wattline lives in the menu bar and shows what's draining your battery, down to the watt, and how much time you'd get back by quitting each app. Drag an app's slice out of the battery to quit it.

It also shows battery health, the charge limit, temperature, Low Power Mode, sleep drain, your AirPods and other accessories, and up to a year of history. Everything stays on your Mac.

This installer puts Wattline in your Applications folder and opens it when it's done. It also adds a one-line rule so Wattline can switch Low Power Mode without asking for your password.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. The charge limit needs Apple silicon and macOS 26.4 or later.
